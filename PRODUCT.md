## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite, React, and CSS. The desk is served by nginx in Docker on port 8420. The API is FastAPI on port 8421.

## Users

A reviewer at a synthetic seat, demonstrated to a hiring panel. The person reads a batch, asks one cited question, and signs or refuses a bounded pack.

## Product Purpose

Desert Ledger is a readiness review for one synthetic UAE tenant. Agents extract invoices, cite frozen FTA and MoF passages, and stop when the evidence fails. A person approves the pack. Nothing is sent to an accredited provider.

## Positioning

The review is a team of checkers, not a document chatbot. The validator is code. The auditor does not see the classifier’s reasoning. A pass is not a compliance claim.

## Operating Context

The reviewer signs in on this desk, loads a seeded batch or uploads a synthetic file, watches the agent trace, asks in Arabic or English, and approves, rejects, or escalates. Research date 2026-11-07. Tenant Desert Ledger Trading LLC, mainland, stated revenue AED 62,000,000, TRN labelled SYNTHETIC_TRN_NOT_VALID.

## Capabilities and Constraints

English and Arabic, with the page direction following the language. Amounts, TRNs, invoice identifiers, and dates stay left-to-right. One repair, then the item stays to verify. Residency is unverified, so only synthetic content is sent. The pack status approved_bounded is not a filing. No savings figure. No live tax number.

## Brand Commitments

The name is Desert Ledger. Arabic: دفتر الصحراء. The split-login theme is retired by the request to replace the UI. Do not present the desk as Stripe, Mercury, or any other company’s product.

## Evidence on Hand

## Product Principles

Show the evidence at the same size as a pass. Name the limit in the same place as the result. Keep the human signature visible. Treat every upload as data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The page is bilingual, including right-to-left layout. Body text must stay readable on its surface. Focus, errors, and the password rules stay visible.
